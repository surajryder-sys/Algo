V6S-ICT is a new standalone MQL5 EA, started 2026-09-27: `v6s_ict/V6S_ICT_EA_v1.50.mq5` (versioned files from v1.50 on, see [[feedback_ea_versioned_files]]), magic 26092701. It is based on the other chat's combined chart indicator `mql5/Dynamic_Zones_CISD_MajorMinor.mq5`; a snapshot copy is in `v6s_ict/`, and it was first compiled here with 0 errors. It is unrelated to [[project_v5s_ict_grid_bot]] (the V5S-ICT grid EA).

Rules, agreed one question at a time:
- Levels: Major/Minor S/R (ZigZag, PivotPeriod 5), ported verbatim from the indicator, on H4, H2, H1, M30, M15, M10, M5 and M3.
- Aligning level: the exact same price on 3+ DIFFERENT timeframes (MinAlignTimeframes=3) AND at least 2 of those TFs have it as a MAJOR level (MinMajorTimeframes=2, v1.40, user 2026-09-27: H1 Maj + M30 Maj + M15 Min valid; M15 Maj + H1 Min + M30 Min not valid). Same side (support or resistance).
- Touch: an M5 candle comes within TouchBufferPrice 2.0 of the level (the user noted price often reverses just short of it).
- Entry: a bullish M5 CISD on a LATER candle than the touch, with that candle closing ABOVE the support, opens a buy (sells mirror this). Valid only while the level is still aligning.
- Size: 0.06 lot. SL = lowest low of the candles AFTER the touch candle, up to the CISD candle, minus a 0.5 buffer.
- Management: at 1:2 the SL moves to breakeven. At 1:7 (PartialR=7, v1.50; was 1:3) it closes everything except 0.01.
- Exit: no fixed TP. An opposite setup at an aligning level closes the trade and reverses it; if the level breaks, the trade keeps running.
- One trade at a time.
- LOSING-STREAK BRAKE (input LossStreakBrake): built and tested (+1,774 over 6 months), but the user turned it OFF (default 0) on 2026-09-27 in favour of:
- M10 AFTER SL (input M10AfterSL=true, v1.30): when a trade from an aligning level hits SL (not a BE stop), THAT level's (side+price) next entry needs an M10 CISD (after a fresh touch, the M10 candle closing beyond the level). All other levels stay on M5 CISD. The flag clears when that level trades again. Later turned OFF (v1.40: M5 CISD only). 6-month sim: 467 trades, +1,227, drop 1,324, lowest -997; the 46 M10 re-entries made +647; monthly Apr -769, May +623, Jun +844, Jul -312, Aug -283, Sep +1,124.
- On-screen panel lists aligning levels with their TF members, touch state, trade status and brake status; the EA auto-loads the Dynamic_Zones_CISD_MajorMinor indicator (tester_indicator) for the visual tester.
- Dynamic Zones are computed and drawn but NOT used in the rules yet (the user will add that later).
- The chart draws everything in the visual tester and live: all TF Major/Minor lines, aligning levels, touches, CISD dots, entry/SL/1:2/1:3 lines, BE and partial markers, and the DZ lines. The user asked for this so they can see how the EA works.
